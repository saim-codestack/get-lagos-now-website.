10 AEO Questions I Should Be Asking?

Published 2026-01-23Updated 2026-01-23By mlagos
Originally published January 23, 2026. Historical platform instructions, statistics, and opinions retain their original context.

In the new world that rides with the regular assistive use of AI there are now platforms that are immersed with the option to use it and with other platforms you simply will be forced to because it’s become the New Way.

However, one of the options you don’t have as an individual/professional/business owner/organizational leader is if the AI Search Engines will be gathering info about all and making it readily available to the public. So now, it’s VERY important to understand the concept that there’s two separate search processes occurring that will need to be influenced and managed going forward – AEO (AI Engine Optimization) and SEO (Search Engine Optimization.

With that being said, let’s look at the TOP 10 AEO Questions You Should Be Asking:

  • What’s the top AI platform(s) that persons would be asking for information about me or my business/org.?
  • What can be done to make sure that AI is answering my/our information correctly?
  • What can I do if it doesn’t?
  • What are the best free or low cost solutions for resolutions to help resolve an issue?
  • What are the best ways to retrain the AI search engines to understand and influence it with the correct info?
  • What legal rights do I or my business have regarding what third-party platforms may publish or share about me, my brand, or my intellectual property?
  • What resources are available to me?
  • What are some of the best basic management AEO practices I should move forward with as a small business?
  • What are some of the more robust optimization solutions and management for medium and larger sized orgs.?
  • What general info should I understand to navigate this new playing field successfully at this stage?
