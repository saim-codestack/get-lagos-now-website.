Published AI & Consulting Policy

AI Policy — Our company specializes in AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) and SEO (Search Engine Optimization) so your site, your company, and your platforms are high performing both technically and within the important search features that are stating your title and company's services, reviews, ideas, references, quotes and definitions — but under no circumstance is AI ever used for website development, coding, functionality or system integrations without full disclosure and agreement. Business as a force for good

WHY Is Get Lagos Now a registered Benefit company?

Get Lagos Now (Lagos Consulting LLC) is registered as a Benefit Company in the State of Oregon and certified by Benefit Corporations for Good (BCFG). We are part of a movement of leaders balancing purpose and profit — using business as a force for good.

We make legal, financial, and operational decisions that consider the impact our work has on our employees, clients, suppliers, communities, and the environment — staying dedicated to helping make an effective impact on the alleviation of local homelessness and expanding digital access.

UN Sustainable Development Goal Aligned with Goal 1: No Poverty — supporting local Portland nonprofits through technology access, volunteerism, and annual giving.
