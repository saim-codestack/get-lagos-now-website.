About Get Lagos Now

Driven by purpose.
Built around people.

We're a Portland-based digital agency bringing people, strategy, and technology together to create meaningful experiences. Since 2010, we've helped organizations grow through thoughtful design, innovation, and purposeful collaboration.

Get to know us

Our journey

A different kind
of digital agency.

Founded in 2010, Get Lagos Now was built around a straightforward belief: digital innovation should create meaningful value for people and organizations.

Today, the agency combines strategic thinking, creative development, marketing expertise, and inclusive technology to help organizations strengthen their digital presence.

From accessible websites to thoughtful digital strategies, the work reflects an ongoing commitment to collaboration, quality, and positive impact.

The person behind the vision

Meet
Michelle Lagos.

Michelle brings a background in marketing, digital strategy, and business leadership to Get Lagos Now. Her approach puts quality service, innovation, and thoughtful collaboration at the heart of the work, connecting the ambitions of clients with the people behind every project.

Her commitment to positive contribution also extends into community leadership. Her nonprofit involvement includes board service with Blanchet House & Farm and the League of Minority Voters, as well as past service on the New Avenues for Youth Employer Advisory Board.
