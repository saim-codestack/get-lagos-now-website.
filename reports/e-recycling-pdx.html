E Recycling PDX

This is an archived community resource. Event dates, schedules, donation instructions, and recipient descriptions reflect the original publication.

Read the published original ↗Historical source material

Both Strategic IT and Get Lagos Now come together yearly to host an the E-Recycling PDX Event with sponsoring companies and organizations, with the purpose of fulfilling a core passion – to help give back to those in need through quality electronic donations.

 Event link details below and highlights of our first years of donating in 2020 -2026

Below is our QR Codes, both Paypal and Venmo for  donations. Thank you! Please remember the $5.00 coffee amount you send our way are the cost of new cords and supplies we need to get these electronics refurbished and given back out to New Avenues for Youth, Blanchet House, Raphael House and Rosehaven.  Thank you!
